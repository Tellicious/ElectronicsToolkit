Radio Time Signal

—

Emulates the long-wave time-code stations used by radio-controlled clocks, transmitting the same time code each real station broadcasts: DCF77 (Germany), WWVB (USA), JJY-40 / JJY-60 (Japan), MSF (UK) and BPC (China). Each is sent in its own time zone.

The watch picks up a magnetic field, not sound: the speaker's voice coil carries the audio current and acts as a tiny antenna. The station frequencies are above what the phone can output directly, so the app plays a clean sine at a sub-multiple and lets the amplifier/coil's harmonic land on the real carrier.

To sync: pick the station, turn the volume to maximum, rest the watch's antenna against the phone speaker, and put the watch into manual-receive mode. Keep this screen in the foreground — locking usually takes a few minutes. Wired earbuds resting on the watch often work better than the speaker.
